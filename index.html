Good to know before booking

How long does laundry take?

Regular orders target 24–48 hours. Family Premium includes priority processing.

Can I add ironing or dry cleaning?

Yes. Add ironing when you book. Dry cleaning requests are reviewed and quoted per item.

How do payments and tracking work?

Choose cash on delivery or try the online payment simulation. Keep your order ID to follow its progress.

How do I report an issue with my order?

Open your order and submit a support request using its order ID. Include the garment and the issue so the team can review it.
